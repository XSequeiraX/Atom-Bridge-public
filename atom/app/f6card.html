No card. On the F6: MENU › SYSTEM › USB › SD Card Reader, then plug the USB cable into the bridge. When done: on the F6 choose Off, unplug, and for the bridge again FRC-8 › connect with the cable unplugged, then plug it in.
▶ bridge headphone output: take the TC cable off the F6 and plug headphones

Reads the card (read-only) and lines every F6 take up with the shoot log by its start timecode: the bridge take number, GOOD / NO-GOOD / MARK, notes and the cameras that rolled. Scene, take, circled, note and track names come from the metadata inside the WAV files.

DELETE moves to the F6_TRASH folder on the card; only EMPTY TRASH deletes for good. Renaming files does not change the metadata inside them (scene, take...). The F6 may not play back takes whose names or folders were changed. PLAY uses the bridge's headphone output; while something plays, rename / move / delete wait (stop it first).
